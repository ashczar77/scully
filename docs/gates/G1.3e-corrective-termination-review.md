## Decisions and tradeoffs

The validator intentionally mirrors the pinned direct client's image-source
schema at the adapter boundary. This is stricter than passing arbitrary image
text through and makes another formatting rejection detectable offline.

Attempt 002 preserves the exact termination operations approved at G1.3c.
Only the request representation and accounting semantics changed. The failure
record for attempt 001 is not rewritten to use the corrected field names.
