## Alternatives and trade-offs

### Keep all records in one project-plan document

This has a lower initial maintenance cost, but decision history and individual
reviews become difficult to locate and audit as the project grows.

### Create every planned document immediately

This makes the intended structure visible, but creates empty files and
premature process overhead.

### Selected approach

Keep PROJECT_PLAN.md as the general source of truth and create specialized
records only when an active step needs them. Approved gate records and ADRs
take precedence over general plan text for their specific subject.
