## Result

Attempt `g1.2-tavily-001` succeeded with five attributable sources and exactly
one reported credit. The redacted evidence is stored in
`validation/results/g1.2-tavily-probe-001.json`.

The results were localized and protocol variants of the same Express proxy
guide. The provider therefore proved relevant source discovery, while also
exposing a later product requirement to canonicalize and deduplicate source
URLs.

## Recommendation

Approve Tavily as a proven sponsor primitive. Authorize implementation and
offline review of the bounded Sandbox capability probe. Do not authorize a
Sandbox operation at this checkpoint.
