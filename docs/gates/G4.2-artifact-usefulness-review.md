## Correctness issue resolved during review

The first minimizer implementation could treat two missing paths as equal for
a `same_value` matcher. The matcher now distinguishes missing data from a
present value and fails closed unless both comparison paths exist. The full
suite and independent archive check passed after the correction.

## Known limitations

1. Automated reduction is limited to observation fields.
2. Structural reductions are guided and recorded rather than exhaustively
   generated.
3. The package covers one selected realistic incident.
4. Broader adversarial export testing remains Step 4.3 work.
5. Clean-checkout and sponsor-stack repeatability remain Step 4.4 work.
6. Direct target-engineer validation and previously recorded provider debt
   remain open.

## Recommendation

Approve Gate G4.2 and authorize Step 4.3 only: harden adversarial capsule,
secret, path, command, export, resource, audit, and inconclusive-result
behavior without enabling live providers or deployment.
