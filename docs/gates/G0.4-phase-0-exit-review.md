## Objective

Use public practitioner evidence and source-grounded proxy analyses to test
whether executable reproduction has credible value beyond explanation alone,
then select one demonstration case and one backup without overstating the
strength of the evidence.

## Scope completed

- Reviewed official guidance on minimal reproductions, environment evidence,
  behavioral comparison, and privacy-safe sharing.
- Reviewed public incident and maintainer evidence for all three candidate
  patterns.
- Constructed three proxy analyses with separate observed and inferred claims.
- Classified the value of executable proof for each proxy lens.
- Scored every eligible candidate against the approved weighted rubric.
- Selected one demonstration case and one backup.
- Recorded direct user validation as evidence debt.

## Tests and validations

- Confirmed the research uses direct official documentation, public issue
  records, and maintainer discussion rather than invented quotations.
- Checked the scoring arithmetic against the seven weighted dimensions.
- Confirmed all three candidates remain eligible under the G0.3 safety and
  reproduction rules.
- Confirmed the selected proxy case retains its `200,429` failing signature and
  `200,200` known-good comparison.
- Checked changed public files for invalid whitespace, prohibited punctuation,
  and unsupported attribution claims.

## Decisions and tradeoffs

- Public research and proxy analysis replace direct interviews for the current
  hackathon gate because participants are not presently accessible.
- The substitute is sufficient for provisional case selection and Phase 1
  feasibility work. It is not evidence of usability, adoption intent, or
  organizational approval.
- Proxy identity collapse is selected because it combines real operational
  evidence, safe branching depth, deterministic behavior, and visual clarity.
- Jackson classpath version skew is the backup because it has strong
  investigation depth but higher setup and explanation costs.
- Pydantic settings migration remains a portability fixture but is not selected
  because its error message already supplies most of the diagnosis.

## Recommendation

problem and the proxy analyses provide a defensible provisional case choice.
Approval should authorize Phase 1 sponsor-stack feasibility while prohibiting
claims that direct interviews, observed preference, usability testing, or
adoption validation occurred.
