# Third-Party Notices

The desktop shell uses Electron (MIT, with Chromium and Node.js notices supplied in its distribution), React and React DOM (MIT), and Lucide icons (ISC).

Development tooling includes Electron Forge, Vite, TypeScript, esbuild, Vitest, Testing Library, jsdom and Playwright. Their versions and dependency trees are recorded in `package-lock.json`. Consult each package's distributed licence for its full terms. Preserve Electron's bundled licence files when distributing the application.

Additional dependency and asset notices will be maintained as components are introduced. Any redistributed third-party software or artwork must retain its applicable licence and attribution requirements. Release-wide transitive licence review remains pending before publication.
